## Unseen evaluation

The step-400 model improved substantially by validation loss, but unseen output remains unstable. Examples include recognizable section labels and occasional prompt-related nouns mixed with malformed or invented words. That is compatible with the requested funny/nonsensical mode, but it is not comparable to a large music model.

## Limitations

Music V1 is a tiny experimental model. It can produce gibberish, miss requested line counts, lose the prompt, and invent music facts. It should be treated as a funny original-lyrics experiment rather than a reliable composer. Copyrighted-lyric requests are rejected by the Music boundary layer; original chaotic writing is allowed.
