`checkpoints/public_games_v3/latest.pt`, final-pass step 800, is selected in `serving_config.json` and loaded on the existing CUDA API. The public `/api/status` endpoint confirmed this selection. ## Data and behavior

The first corpus contained 484 focus conversations and 630 replay conversations. The final binding corpus contains 716 focus conversations, including natural question phrasings. Topics include Gorilla Tag, modding communities, Unity/BepInEx/Harmony, Minecraft, Roblox, the ChudGPT model family, geography, awareness, and conversation. Review filters reject malformed roles, prompt leaks, private-log fields, wrong assistant personas, duplicates, empty/generic nonanswers, and excessive repetition. Initial assembly rejected six persona examples and 36 duplicates. See `games_dataset_audit.json` locally and the source notes in `GAMES_RETRAIN_SOURCES.md`.

All 1,519 records in both provided Discord JSONL files were parsed for the earlier regression audit. Raw logs, message IDs, and personal identifiers were not added to the dataset or published.

Game responses remain generated by the model. No generated-answer lookup, invented-model detector, or generic apology replacement was added. Existing narrow geography, awareness, emoji, identity, and greeting routes remain transparent in `assistance_reason`. Supported repeated grounded replies vary within a session; exact math and generated output are left alone. Variation is limited to supported formats, not a claim that every possible reply is unique. Humor is occasional, not required in every answer.

## Evaluation and limitations

The 26-prompt raw comparison bypasses all grounded handlers; 23 cases have rough keyword checks and three require manual inspection. Recovery-v5 scored 1/23, games v2 step 800 scored 13/23, and selected games v3 scored 17/23 with greedy decoding. Sampled v3 scored 14/23 (v2 final sampled scored 15/23). This is a targeted development check, not a general accuracy benchmark. These prompts informed iteration; related paraphrases and answers can cross the validation split. Lower loss and keyword presence do not establish correctness or generalization.

The live serving check verified neural replies for “What is Gorilla Tag?” and “Describe the Gorilla Tag modding scene.” Their replies correctly described VR hand/arm locomotion and the community's programmers, artists, map makers, testers, plugins, compatibility, and debugging. The assistance flag was false for both.

Failures remain visible: the raw model mishandled a GTAG paraphrase, returned an even-number function instead of doubling, confused arithmetic with ChudGPT Plus, and inserted “capital” into a Fabric explanation. Fresh live questions about Unity mod conflicts and testers went off-topic. The family answer could truncate with a 120-token budget. Unknown fabricated model names still receive unreliable generated output; nothing replaces that output.

Live repeat checks produced “The capital of Australia is Canberra,” “Canberra is the capital of Australia,” and “For Australia, the capital is Canberra.” Greetings and disclosures varied, and pride-flag meaning preserved the distinction between symbol use and personal identity.
